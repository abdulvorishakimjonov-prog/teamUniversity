Internships for Students

The TEAM internship programme combines practical work experience with structured learning. Each placement is built around a specific brief that meets the goals of both the employer and the university. It gives students the opportunity to apply their knowledge, develop professional skills, explore career paths, and gain valuable experience in a real business environment.

OUR MISSION

A Supportive Environment for Every Studen

Our mission is to support experiential learning alongside entrepreneurial education and to increase the number and quality of internships that lead to stable jobs for graduates.
Through practical work experience, students can connect their academic knowledge with real business challenges, understand workplace expectations, and prepare for their future careers. The programme also aims to create meaningful cooperation between TEAM University and employers.
