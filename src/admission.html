Pre-Foundation course entry requirements

Education Requirements

Applicants must have studied a broad range of subjects and hold a secondary education certificate.

For applicants educated in Uzbekistan, accepted equivalents include:

  • Certificate of Secondary Education (O'rta Ma'lumot To'g'risida Attestat)
  • Diploma of Specialised Secondary Education (Academic Lyceum Diploma)
  • Vocational Education Diploma (Vocational College Diploma)

International students will be considered based on an assessment of their submitted documents.

English Language Requirements

Applicants must meet one of the following requirements:

  • English at secondary school or GCSE level with a grade C or above
  • Achieve 40% of the passing mark in the TEAM English Test
  • IELTS 5.5, with at least 5.0 in Writing
  • Completion of a Pre-Foundation, Foundation or higher-level English language programme recognised by TEAM

Accepted IELTS equivalents:

  • Cambridge English — Overall 162, Writing 154
  • TOEFL iBT — Overall 72, Writing 14
  • Duolingo — 90
  • Alternative certificates are considered individually.
Age Requirements

Applicants must normally be 16 years old by 1 October of the academic year in which they apply.

The process of checking and verifying applications and documents of applicants takes 5 working days from the date of submission.
