Visa Type & TEAM Invitation Process

International applicants admitted to TEAM University receive a Student Visa (Category A-2) issued for higher education studies in Uzbekistan.

How TEAM Supports Your Invitation:

International applicants admitted to TEAM University receive a Student Visa (Category A-2) issued for higher education studies in Uzbekistan.

Visa Telex Invitation: TEAM International Office submits your passport and offer letter to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) of Uzbekistan. Embassy Notification: Once the MFA approves the telex number, you apply at the nearest Uzbek Embassy or Consulate in your home country. Local Registration Support: Upon arrival in Tashkent, TEAM assists with mandatory temporary residence registration (propiska).
