TEAM University offers a Master's Degree in International Business at the prestigious University of St Andrews in the UK. Students gain practical skills through real-world projects, industry partnerships and global exposure that prepare them for careers in leading international companies.

The programme combines academic rigour with hands-on experience, covering strategy, finance, marketing, operations and leadership. Graduates join a worldwide alumni network and benefit from dedicated career support throughout their studies.

Admissions are open to applicants with a strong academic record and a motivation to develop their international careers. Scholarships and tuition support options are available for outstanding candidates from partner countries.

Participants also work closely with faculty members and visiting practitioners, taking part in workshops, case competitions and study trips that connect classroom learning with the realities of modern business.
