Pre-Foundation

Your journey to TEAM in five clear steps.

Academic requirements

Successfully complete International Foundation Course. Complete 1st year at a recognised higher education institution. GCE A-level qualifications (excluding native language) + 3 GCSEs grade C or above (incl. English & Math). Equivalent UK Bachelor entry qualification (e.g., International Baccalaureate - IB).

Minimum age

18 years by 31 October of the entry year.

English proficiency

IELTS 6.0 (min 5.5 in Writing) OR GCSE Grade C in English OR Foundation Academic English min 40%.
