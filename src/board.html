Role of the board

The Board provides strategic oversight and ensures that TEAM University remains aligned with its vision, values and academic standards.

Strategy

Vision, Mission, Values and long-term strategy.

Leadership

Appoints and oversees senior university executives, including the Rector and Deputies.

Finance

Approves the overall budget and provides financial oversight.

Risk & Policies

Oversees risk management and key institutional policies.

Academic Governance

Protects academic independence and maintains high academic standards.

Accountability

Ensures the University's decisions and activities remain aligned with its mission and values.
