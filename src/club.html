EMBA Alumni Club

The EMBA Alumni Club brings together graduates of the Executive MBA programme delivered by TEAM University and Kozminski University. It provides a space for business owners and senior executives to stay connected, exchange professional experiences, and explore opportunities for continued learning and collaboration beyond the programme.

Who can Join?

The EMBA Alumni Club is intended for graduates of the Executive MBA programme delivered by TEAM University in partnership with Kozminski University.
It brings together business owners and senior executives who want to maintain professional relationships, share experiences, and remain connected with their fellow graduates.

Eligibility Criteria:

Graduates of the TEAM University × Kozminski University Executive MBA programme.
